How We Protect Your Privacy

Updated November 2026

1. Simple Summary

Your privacy is important to us. We only collect the information you choose to give us (like your name, email, and phone number when you fill out our contact form) so we can answer your questions and work on your project.

2. What We Collect

  • Your name and contact details when you reach out to us.
  • Information you provide about your business and project goals.
  • Basic, anonymous website visit numbers to see which pages people find helpful.

3. What We Never Do

We will never sell, rent, or give away your email address, phone number, or project details to spammers, telemarketers, or outside advertisers.

4. Security

We use secure website encryption (HTTPS) to make sure your messages reach us safely.

5. Questions?

If you ever want us to delete your contact details from our records, simply send an email to privacy@truetech.agency and we will take care of it immediately.
